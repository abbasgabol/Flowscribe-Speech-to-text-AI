LOCAL DICTATION 0.4.0
Your voice. Your computer.

This installer includes the app, its Python runtime, and local speech models.
No separate Python installation, account, API key, or setup download is needed.

Choose your installation location on the next pages. You may select C:, G:, or
another writable drive. The program and models are installed in that location.

Includes the base.en English model and punctuation refinement.
Both run on your CPU. The larger small.en model is not bundled.
An 8 GB Windows 10/11 x64 PC is the recommended starting point. Speed varies.

HOW TO USE
Click in a text field, hold Win + Alt, speak, and release to insert the text.
The button inside the app records into its preview instead. Esc cancels recording.
Closing the window hides it to the tray. Choose Quit to exit fully.

PRIVACY AND STORAGE
Audio is processed locally in memory, not saved or sent to a server.
Only explicitly saved corrections and your model preference are remembered.
These small files are stored separately in %LOCALAPPDATA%\LocalDictation.
They remain after uninstall so reinstalling does not erase your preferences.
Delete that folder yourself if you also want to remove your saved corrections.

Recognition and punctuation can be wrong. Review important text before using it.
No automatic grammar rewriting or conversation-history learning is included.

UNINSTALL
Use Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > Local Dictation > Uninstall.
The uninstaller removes installed application files, models, and shortcuts.
User preferences are preserved as described above.
